# Privacy Policy for Sanoor

**Effective Date:** May 12, 2026

At Eonastra, we prioritize the privacy and security of you and your baby. This Privacy Policy explains how the **Sanoor** mobile application ("App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Local Data Storage & Privacy-First Architecture
Sanoor is built with a privacy-first architecture. All of the personal data you enter into the App—including your baby's name, birthdate, feeding logs, sleep records, diaper changes, growth measurements, and photos—is **stored locally on your device**. 

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not transmit, sync, store, or sell this personal data on our servers. Your data belongs to you.

## 2. Information We Collect Automatically
To help us maintain and improve Sanoor, we use third-party services that automatically collect anonymous usage and performance data:

*   **Crash Data (Firebase Crashlytics):** If the App crashes, we collect anonymous diagnostic data (such as device type, OS version, and the line of code that caused the crash) to help us fix bugs rapidly.
*   **Analytics (Firebase Analytics):** We collect anonymous usage statistics (such as which screens are viewed or which features are most popular) to understand how the App is used and to guide future updates.
*   **Advertising (Google AdMob):** We may display advertisements in the App using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers) and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt-out of personalized advertising in your device's privacy settings or via the in-app "Ads & Privacy Settings" option.
*   **Subscription Management (RevenueCat):** We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes your purchase history and subscription status. This data is transmitted securely and is used solely to verify and manage your subscription entitlements. RevenueCat does not have access to your personal baby tracking data.

**Crucially, none of this automated data is linked to your identity, your name, or the personal data of your baby.**

## 3. How We Use This Information
We use the automatically collected anonymous data solely to:
*   Diagnose and fix technical issues and crashes.
*   Analyze usage trends to improve the App's features and user experience.
*   Provide relevant advertisements to support the development of Sanoor.
*   Verify and manage your subscription status.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all personal data is stored locally on your device, **we do not have access to delete it for you**. 
*   **To delete your personal data:** You can use the "Delete All Data" option in the App's Profile settings, or simply uninstall the Sanoor app from your device. Either action will immediately and permanently delete all local data associated with the App.
*   **Anonymous Data Retention:** Anonymous crash and analytics data retained by our third-party providers (Google Firebase) is kept in accordance with their respective retention policies, typically up to 14 months.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)
Even though we do not collect or process personally identifiable information (PII) on our servers, we respect your global privacy rights:
*   **Right to Access / Deletion:** Because data is local, you have full control and access to your data at all times.
*   **Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising:** You may opt out of personalized ads by utilizing your device settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android), or by using the "Ads & Privacy Settings" option within the App.

## 6. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party service providers, which have their own privacy policies:

*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)
*   [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Sanoor is a tool designed strictly for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. All data regarding children is inputted by the adult user and remains strictly local to the device. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, if applicable, providing a notice within the App.
